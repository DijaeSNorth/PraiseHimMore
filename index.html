Our Role

Praise Him More is a not-for-profit gospel radio station sharing gospel artists whose music praises the name of GOD. Praise Him More is a promotional and feedback platform, not a record label, publisher, distributor, rights administrator, or representative of submitting artists.

Artists retain their copyrights and ownership. A submission does not transfer rights, guarantee feedback, promotion, broadcast, placement, or acceptance. Submitters must own or control all necessary rights to the music and media they provide.

Praise Him More reserves full discretion to review, decline, remove, or deny any submission for any reason, including content that does not align with the station's gospel mission.
